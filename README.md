# honey app: how coupon hunting, rewards, and cashback actually work

Searching for **honey app** usually means one of three things: you want an app that finds coupon codes automatically, you want price-drop alerts while shopping online, or you want some form of cashback after checkout.

PayPal Honey covers the first two jobs particularly well. It can search for available promo codes, apply eligible offers at checkout, show shopping deals, and notify US members when selected items in their Droplist fall in price. The mobile app is currently available in the US, UK, Australia, Canada, Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands, India, and Spain.

There is one important distinction, though: **Honey and ShopBack are different services**. Honey focuses on coupon discovery, shopping offers, price tracking, and rewards. ShopBack is primarily a cashback platform. The link used in this article leads to ShopBack US, so it is best understood as a cashback alternative or companion to Honey rather than the Honey app itself.

## What is the Honey app?

Honey is a shopping assistant from PayPal. Its browser extension and mobile app help shoppers look for promotions before completing an online order.

The main Honey features include:

- Searching for available coupon codes at checkout
- Applying eligible codes automatically
- Showing offers from participating retailers
- Tracking selected products for price drops
- Comparing prices on some Amazon listings
- Earning rewards on eligible purchases
- Accessing shopping offers through the mobile app

PayPal describes the browser extension as a free tool that searches available coupon codes across more than 30,000 popular sites. When you reach checkout, Honey can test available codes and apply an eligible offer. That does not mean every order will receive a discount. A code may be expired, restricted to certain products, limited to new customers, or unavailable for the exact store page you are using.

The mobile app works slightly differently from a traditional coupon website. US members can use the in-app shopping experience to browse participating stores, view available offers, save products to Droplist, and receive price-drop notifications where supported. Honey also says that members can view and apply recently successful promo codes before checkout.

In practical terms, Honey is most useful when you are already ready to buy. It can reduce the amount of time spent opening several coupon websites and manually testing codes one by one. It is less useful as a guarantee of a specific discount because the available offers depend on the retailer, product, account, location, and checkout conditions.

## Is the Honey app free?

Yes. Honey’s shopping extension and rewards program do not require a paid subscription. PayPal states that the Honey extension is free, and PayPal Rewards does not charge an additional sign-up fee.

That does not mean every feature works everywhere. Honey rewards are available only on eligible purchases and participating merchants. Rewards may also depend on whether you activate the offer correctly before checkout.

The basic cost structure looks like this:

| Service | Price | What you receive |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Honey browser extension | Free | Coupon search, offer discovery, and checkout assistance |
| Honey mobile app | Free | Shopping offers, price tracking, and eligible rewards |
| PayPal Rewards through Honey | No additional membership fee | Points on eligible offers and transactions |
| ShopBack US | Free | Cashback through participating retailers, app, website, or extension |

ShopBack also presents its US service as free, with no sign-up fee, service fee, or withdrawal fee for PayPal or ACH withdrawals according to its current US fee information.

That gives shoppers two different saving models:

- **Honey:** find coupons and earn rewards on eligible activity.
- **ShopBack:** start shopping through ShopBack and receive cashback when the purchase tracks and is confirmed.

Neither system guarantees a discount on every order. The retailer’s terms still control the final result.

## Honey app features worth knowing

### Automatic coupon search

The feature most people associate with Honey is automatic coupon testing. After you install the browser extension and reach a supported checkout page, Honey can search its available coupon database and test codes.

If a valid code applies, the discount should appear in the retailer’s cart or checkout summary. The final amount still needs to be checked manually because some codes may apply only to selected products, exclude shipping, or require a minimum purchase.

A sensible workflow is:

1. Add the items you want to the retailer’s cart.
2. Open the checkout page.
3. Let Honey search for available codes.
4. Review the resulting price.
5. Check whether shipping, tax, or product exclusions changed the calculation.

Honey’s own help information makes clear that rewards are not offered at every store it supports. When rewards are available, users may need to activate them before completing the order.

### Droplist price tracking

US members can save products to Honey’s Droplist and receive notifications when Honey finds a price drop. This is useful for products that are not urgent purchases, such as electronics, home items, clothing, or gifts.

Price tracking is not the same as a full price-history guarantee. A price can change because of:

- A temporary retailer promotion
- A different seller
- A different product size or configuration
- Shipping costs
- Membership pricing
- A limited-time offer
- A change in stock availability

For expensive purchases, compare the exact model, seller, shipping terms, return policy, and final checkout price. A lower headline price is not automatically the better deal.

### Amazon price comparison

Honey can show price information for some Amazon products and compare sellers where the feature is available. This can help when the same item is listed by multiple sellers.

The comparison should still be treated as a shopping aid rather than a final verdict. Check the seller rating, fulfillment method, delivery date, warranty, and return terms before ordering. The cheapest listing is occasionally the one with the least attractive delivery or support conditions.

### Rewards and points

Honey rewards are now connected with PayPal Rewards in relevant markets. Eligible purchases can generate points, and points may be redeemed through PayPal checkout or for rewards such as gift cards, depending on the member’s country and the available redemption options.

The important limitations are easy to miss:

- Rewards are not available at every store.
- Eligible purchases may exclude taxes and shipping.
- The reward rate can vary by retailer and offer.
- Returns or cancellations can affect eligibility.
- Points may take time to appear.
- Redemption options vary by market.

Honey says reward transactions generally appear as pending within 2 to 14 days after an eligible purchase, although timing may vary by retailer and transaction.

So the correct expectation is not “every purchase earns cash.” The more accurate version is: **eligible purchases may earn rewards when the offer is activated and the transaction meets the retailer’s conditions.**

## Honey app versus ShopBack

Honey and ShopBack overlap because both can help shoppers save money, but they are built around different actions.

| Feature | Honey | ShopBack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary purpose | Coupon discovery and shopping assistance | Cashback from participating retailers |
| Browser extension | Yes | Yes |
| Mobile app | Yes, in supported countries | Yes |
| Coupon search | Core feature | May show retailer promotions and approved codes |
| Price-drop tracking | Available for US members through Droplist | Not the main focus |
| Reward format | PayPal Rewards points or available redemption options | Cashback in USD for eligible US activity |
| Withdrawal | Depends on PayPal Rewards and local redemption options | PayPal or ACH, subject to confirmation and account requirements |
| Membership price | Free | Free |
| Best use case | Testing coupons at checkout | Starting a shopping session through a cashback portal |

ShopBack says shoppers should search for the retailer, click through from ShopBack, complete the purchase in the same tracked session, and then wait for the cashback to move from pending to confirmed.

That tracking step matters. If you visit the retailer directly, switch browsers, use an unapproved coupon code, or complete checkout after another affiliate link overwrites the tracking session, the cashback may not register. ShopBack’s support information specifically warns that cashback can fail to track when shoppers use unapproved promo codes, click external cashback links, or switch devices during checkout.

### Which one should you use?

Choose Honey when:

- You mainly want automatic coupon testing.
- You want to track selected products for possible price drops.
- You prefer shopping through a browser extension.
- You are comfortable checking whether a code actually applied.
- You want access to PayPal-related rewards on eligible offers.

Choose ShopBack when:

- You want cashback rather than only a coupon.
- The retailer is listed in ShopBack’s marketplace.
- You are making a larger purchase where cashback can add up.
- You want to withdraw confirmed cashback to PayPal or a US bank account.
- You are willing to begin the shopping session through ShopBack.

You can also use both in the same general shopping routine, but attribution rules matter. Starting at ShopBack, clicking to the retailer, and then allowing another extension or cashback service to take over can affect which platform receives the referral. For a clean transaction, use one cashback platform per order and follow the retailer-specific conditions shown before checkout.

## How to use the Honey app properly

The basic Honey workflow is straightforward, but small details affect the result.

### On a computer

1. Install the Honey browser extension.
2. Visit a participating retailer.
3. Add products to your cart.
4. Go to checkout.
5. Open Honey when the extension appears.
6. Let it search for available codes.
7. Confirm that the discount is visible in the retailer’s final total.

Do not assume that a code was applied just because Honey tested it. The retailer’s checkout page should show the actual discount.

### On mobile

The Honey mobile app is available in selected markets, including the United States. Users can browse participating stores, view available offers, and use the mobile shopping experience where supported.

For a price-drop purchase, save the item to Droplist and wait for an alert. When the alert arrives, check the current product page rather than relying only on the notification. Inventory, seller, shipping, and product configuration may have changed.

For ShopBack, the mobile workflow is different:

1. Open the ShopBack app.
2. Search for the retailer.
3. Review the displayed cashback rate and terms.
4. Tap the retailer’s shopping button.
5. Complete the purchase in the same session.
6. Wait for the transaction to become confirmed.
7. Withdraw when the account meets the payout requirement.

ShopBack describes the same basic activation process through its website and extension: search for the store, click through, and finish checkout without breaking the tracked session.

## Common reasons Honey or cashback does not work

Coupon and cashback tools are useful, but they are not magic checkout buttons. Several predictable issues can prevent savings.

### The code is restricted

A coupon may require:

- A minimum order value
- A specific product category
- A new customer account
- A particular payment method
- A regional shipping address
- A promotional period
- Exclusion of sale items or gift cards

Read the retailer’s terms if the discount disappears at the final step.

### The store does not offer rewards

Honey rewards do not apply to every merchant. A store may support coupon search but not rewards, or it may offer rewards only for selected products. Honey recommends checking whether a rewards offer appears in the extension before completing checkout.

### Cashback tracking was interrupted

For ShopBack, switching devices or browsers, clicking another cashback link, using an unapproved code, or leaving the tracked session can break attribution. Returns and cancellations can also reverse pending cashback.

### The reward is still pending

Cashback and rewards often appear after the retailer reports the transaction. ShopBack separates pending cashback from confirmed cashback, and only confirmed funds are eligible for withdrawal. Retail transactions may take time because the merchant needs to verify that the order was not cancelled or returned.

### The final price is higher than expected

Always compare the final checkout total, not only the coupon amount or cashback percentage. Taxes, shipping, service charges, membership fees, and product exclusions can change the real value of the offer.

## Is the Honey app worth using?

For shoppers who frequently buy online, Honey can be useful because it automates a repetitive task: checking whether a coupon exists before payment. The price is low because the basic service is free, and the extension can save time even when it does not produce a discount on every order.

Its strongest use case is convenience. Install it once, let it check supported checkout pages, and verify the final price yourself.

ShopBack makes more sense when your priority is cashback. It does not replace Honey’s coupon-testing function, but it can return part of an eligible purchase as confirmed cashback when you start the order through the correct tracked path.

A practical approach is:

- Use Honey to check for coupons.
- Use ShopBack when its retailer rate and conditions are attractive.
- Avoid stacking multiple cashback services on one order.
- Review exclusions before buying.
- Confirm the final checkout price.
- Keep order details until rewards or cashback are confirmed.

For a free shopping tool, that is a reasonable amount of effort. The main rule is simple: **a displayed offer is not the same as a completed saving. Check what actually applied before you pay.**
